Sych Automation

Independent, privately operated automation. Sych Automation is a self-hosted reporting workflow used by its operator to organize advertising analytics, Google Drive exports, and Google Sheets dashboards.

This OAuth integration enables access to Google services for accounts that explicitly authorize it. The system is not a publicly offered application.

What it does

  • Reads and updates Google Sheets reports and dashboards when authorized.
  • Accesses Google Drive files and folders for scheduled reporting workflows when authorized.
  • Uses basic Google account information for account identification as allowed by the granted permissions.

Access and privacy

Access is granted through Google's OAuth consent process. An account owner can revoke authorization at Google Account connections.

Read the Privacy Policy for information on the integration's Google user data practices.
