Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026.

This Privacy Policy describes how the privately operated Sych Automation Google OAuth integration handles data for the accounts that authorize its use.

Information accessed

With explicit OAuth authorization, the integration may access Google Drive file content and metadata, Google Sheets data, and the Google account's basic identifying details, such as email address, according to the scopes granted on Google's consent screen.

Purpose of processing

Data is accessed for internal reporting and automation: transferring and organizing exports, updating spreadsheets and dashboards, and checking the status of reporting workflows. Google user data is not sold or used for unrelated advertising targeting.

Storage and retention

Credentials, generated reports, operational logs, and cached records may be stored within the operator's self-hosted environment and configured backups. Stored data is retained for operational reporting, debugging, or recovery while it is needed, and may be deleted by the operator. Revoking OAuth permission stops future API access but does not itself erase exported files, historical reports, or backups.

Sharing and third-party processing

Google APIs are used to perform authorized operations. Where the operator explicitly enables AI-assisted reporting or other integrations, selected reporting content may be processed by those configured providers. Google user data is not sold or shared with data brokers.

Security and control

The operator manages access to the automation host and its credentials. You may revoke this application's access at any time in Google Account connections. For questions or requests to delete stored information, contact the application administrator using the support contact shown on the Google OAuth consent screen.

Website analytics

These informational pages do not include third-party analytics scripts or submission forms. GitHub Pages, the hosting provider, may process technical requests according to its own terms.

Updates

This policy may be amended if the automation's operations change. The effective date above indicates its current version.
